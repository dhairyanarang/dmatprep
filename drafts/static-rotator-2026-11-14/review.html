Static rotator pilot — visual review

SIX DRAFT ITEMS. NOT IN THE BANK. NOT APPROVED. They test staticRotatingSymbol — a symbol that holds its cell and turns — which Exercises 3 and 6 attest by carrying a rotation clause with no movement clause. It is filed under FS_CONTRACT.review, so every item built on it is reasonable_extrapolation and says so. This file is not part of the application, is not a route, and does not reach the production build. Answer keys are shown because this is a reviewer's view.
Existing evidence, which is not approval. An independent blind round on Claude Sonnet 5 — a different model from the author's — was given the four shown matrices and the three options per image as structured data, with keys, rules and explanations withheld and verified absent before dispatch. It chose 12 of 12 continuations in agreement with the keys and judged exactlyOneDefensibleContinuation true on all six. It reported checking and ruling out a second direction reading, an accelerating reading, a moved-and-returned reading and a reflection reading, and rated all six easy.

Two models agreeing is evidence about these items. It is not a person taking responsibility for putting them in front of a candidate, and this sheet exists so that a person can.

Measured, not eyeballed: both the production renderer and this one rotate about the cell centre. The arrow polygon is slightly asymmetric about its origin, so its bounding box does shift under rotation — by 1.5% of a cell at every orientation, about 0.5px on a 34px cell. That is the only positional artefact, and it is nowhere near a one-cell move.
