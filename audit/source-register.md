## Implications for our content

1. **The ~60-question GAM figure is new and quantifies our gap.** Our GAM has **19** active questions against an official average of ~60. Any claim that our GAM is a full-length simulation is unsupportable. The Test page's "Full timed session … under the module's official 90-minute clock" needs qualifying.
2. **The FAQ's 2026 dates are stale**, and our product currently counts down to those dates. Both the product and the FAQ are behind; only the India page is current.
3. **The affected-fields discrepancy persists** and must not be silently resolved.
4. **The transitional rule's connective must be confirmed verbatim** before any eligibility copy ships.
