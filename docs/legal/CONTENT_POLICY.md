# Content, copyright and provenance policy

Applies to every question, explanation, diagram, asset and piece of copy added
to dMAT Prep from 18 November 2026.

**It is a working policy, not legal advice.** Following it reduces risk; it does
not establish that any particular use is lawful. The open questions are in
`docs/legal/LEGAL_BRIEF.md`.

## 1. The rules

### 1.1 Independent creation

Every question, explanation and diagram is created for dMAT Prep. It is built
from the examination formats that g.a.s.t. describes publicly, and from general
reasoning principles — never from the examination itself.

### 1.2 Never from a live examination

**Prohibited without exception**, and no deadline or content gap justifies any
of it:

- Questions recalled from a sat examination, by anyone.
- Questions reconstructed from a recollection, a forum post, a coaching
  handout, or another preparation provider's material.
- Passages, figures, tables or datasets taken from the examination.
- Any of the above with the names, numbers, wording, diagram or options
  changed. **Changing the surface of copied material does not make it
  original.** It makes it a derivative work with the evidence filed off.

A candidate recollection is not a source, not a blueprint, and not a sanity
check. It does not enter the repository in any form.

### 1.3 No copying or close paraphrase of official material

Official wording is not reproduced. Where a *fact* from the official material is
needed, it is restated in our own words and the page is cited for the fact.

Two gates enforce this and must not be weakened:

- `scripts/test/official-wording.test.mjs` fails if any section guide
  reproduces an official 8-gram, or presents anything as a direct quotation.
- The only permitted overlap anywhere is the duration discrepancy in
  `content/exam/claims.ts`, where the conflicting wordings *are* the evidence
  for the claim. That exception is named in the test, so a second one cannot be
  added by accident.

Publicly available does not mean free to reproduce. The preparatory PDF is
downloadable by anyone and is still copyrighted.

### 1.4 Provenance is recorded, not asserted

Every question carries a provenance record computed from the question itself by
`scripts/lib/provenance.mjs`. `verifyQuestion` rejects any stored grade the
question does not earn, so provenance cannot be improved by editing a field.

Each record states where the content came from (`originality`), what the
candidate is shown (`format`), how they answer (`answerFormat`,
`answerOptionCount`) and what the item tests (`mechanics`).

### 1.5 Third-party assets are recorded with their licence

No asset enters the repository until its source and licence are written down in
`docs/IP_AUDIT.md`. That includes icons, fonts, illustrations, photographs,
device mockups, UI kits, and anything exported from a Figma Community file or a
generator.

"It came from our Figma" is not a provenance record. The question is what the
Figma file was built from.

**Never used:** official logos, wordmarks, colour schemes or visual branding, in
any context that could imply endorsement.

### 1.6 Review before publishing

No new question reaches a candidate on automated checks alone. The gates in
`npm run verify:content` are necessary and not sufficient; `npm run
release:check` audits containment over the build. Module B additionally requires
a person's sign-off, which it has not had.

### 1.7 Removing or quarantining disputed content

When content is challenged, or an internal finding casts doubt on it:

1. **Quarantine first, decide after.** Set `meta.quarantined` with a reason and
   a date. The item stays in the file and stays resolvable by id — attempts
   already filed against it must keep naming their question — but
   `lib/content/registry.ts` excludes it from every pool, and
   `scripts/build-solutions.mjs` stops publishing its solution.
2. **Rebuild and verify.** `npm run build:solutions && npm run release:check`
   confirms the item's id, prose and solution are absent from the build.
3. **Retire only when the decision is final.** Retirement reserves the id
   forever and archives the payload. Quarantine is reversible; retirement is
   not.
4. **Never rewrite a committed question in place.** Attempt and exposure records
   are filed against its id, and rewriting under an unchanged id silently
   changes what those records mean.

Both states are recorded in `audit/content-register.md`, regenerated by
`npm run register`.

## 2. If someone says we have infringed their rights

### 2.1 The route in

Published at `/about`:

> If you believe something here infringes your rights, write to
> **dhairyanarang077@gmail.com**. Reports are acknowledged within three working
> days and the material in question is withdrawn from the site while it is
> looked into.

### 2.2 What happens, and when

| Step | Target | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Acknowledge | 3 working days | Confirm receipt, name a reference, ask for anything missing. No admission or denial. |
| 2. Quarantine | Immediately on a specific, credible claim | Follow §1.7. Withdrawing material while a claim is examined is not an admission, and it is said in those words. |
| 3. Investigate | 14 days | Trace the material through its provenance record, generator stamp, originality-corpus result and git history. Produce a dated written finding. |
| 4. Decide | 21 days | Restore, permanently retire, or seek a licence. Record the reason either way. |
| 5. Respond | With the decision | Explain what was done. If restoring, give the evidence. |
| 6. Record | Always | Every complaint and its outcome, in an append-only log, whatever the result. |

### 2.3 What a complainant should send

The material complained of (URL or question id), what right is claimed and the
basis for it, what outcome is sought, and how to reply. A specific identification
is what allows step 2; a general assertion does not.

### 2.4 What is not promised

The process does not promise to agree, to pay, or to keep material down
permanently. It promises to look, to look quickly, and to say what was found.

### 2.5 Not yet published, and why

This process is **drafted, not live**. Before it is published, a lawyer must
settle:

1. Whether a **dedicated address** is required rather than a personal mailbox —
   the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 require a published
   grievance mechanism for an Indian seller, and a personal Gmail is a poor
   choice for one once money changes hands.
2. Whether a named **grievance officer** with a postal address must be
   published, and whether these timelines satisfy the Rules.
3. Whether any **safe-harbour** regime is available to a first-party publisher
   of its own content, and whether the wording should reflect that it is not an
   intermediary.
4. Whether **automatic withdrawal on complaint** is wise, or whether it invites
   abuse and should be qualified.
5. What the response should say about **jurisdiction** when the complainant is
   outside India.

## 3. Adding anything new — the checklist

| Before publishing | Check |
|---|---|
| Is every item independently created, from documented formats only? | §1.1 |
| Can anyone involved point to a recollection, a competitor's material or a forum post? | §1.2 — stop |
| Is any official wording reproduced? | §1.3 — `npm test` |
| Does every item carry a derived provenance record? | §1.4 — `verify:bank` |
| Is every new asset's source and licence written down? | §1.5 — `docs/IP_AUDIT.md` |
| Have the gates been run, and has a person looked? | §1.6 |
| Does the build contain nothing quarantined, retired or draft? | `npm run release:check` |

## 4. Review

This policy is reviewed when the examination's terms change, when counsel
answers any question in the legal brief, before the first paid transaction, and
after any infringement complaint. Changes are recorded in git rather than
edited silently.
