## The short version

**Seven days, no questions asked, provided you have used less than 20% of the
paid material.** Beyond that we look at it case by case.

## 1. What you are buying

A one-time purchase of {{PRICE}} giving access to the paid material on dMAT
Prep. It is not a subscription, nothing renews, and there is nothing to cancel.
You will not be charged again.

## 2. Cancelling before you buy

Close the checkout. Nothing is taken until the payment completes.

## 3. Refunds

### 3.1 The seven-day window

Ask within **seven days** of purchase and we refund in full, without asking why,
provided you have attempted **less than 20%** of the paid questions.

The usage condition exists because this is digital material that cannot be
returned. It is stated up front rather than applied as a surprise.

### 3.2 Beyond seven days, or beyond 20%

Write to us anyway. We will refund in full where:

- the paid material was not delivered, or was inaccessible for a sustained
  period through our fault;
- you were charged more than once for the same purchase;
- a question you paid for turns out to be wrong and we cannot correct it; or
- we withdraw paid material during the period you paid for — refunded in
  proportion to what remains.

Outside those, a refund is at our discretion and we will give you a reason
either way.

### 3.3 When we will not refund

- A changed mind after substantial use, outside the seven-day window.
- An examination result. **We do not guarantee any outcome and a disappointing
  score is not a defect in the service.**
- An account suspended for sharing credentials or redistributing our material.

## 4. How to ask

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the address on your account, with the date of
purchase and the payment reference. Nothing else is needed — there is no form.

## 5. How long it takes

We acknowledge within **48 hours** and decide within **seven working days**.

An approved refund is sent to the original payment method. Once we release it,
the money takes **5–10 working days** to appear, depending on your bank or card
issuer. That part is outside our control and we cannot speed it up.

## 6. Failed and duplicate payments

If money left your account but access was not granted, write to us — do not pay
again. A failed payment is usually reversed automatically by the aggregator
within **5–7 working days**. A duplicate charge is refunded in full, and the
seven-day window does not apply to it.

## 7. Grievances

Unhappy with a refund decision: {{GRIEVANCE_OFFICER}}, our grievance officer
under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
Acknowledged within 48 hours, resolved within one month.
