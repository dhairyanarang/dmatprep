## 8. Payments

Specification only — `docs/PAYMENTS.md`. Nothing implemented.

**Recommendation: Cashfree, as an Individual, domestic-only.** Its documentation
blesses the exact individual→proprietorship path, it states GST is not mandatory
in those words, and — decisive, because entitlement is granted on a webhook —
it is the only one documenting at-least-once delivery with an idempotency
header. Razorpay **disables a webhook after 24 hours of failures**, which is a
silent outage with a paid customer locked out.

**Stripe is out on three verified facts**, the decisive one being that it
supports neither UPI nor netbanking in India.

**Architectural blockers before any implementation:** every page is statically
prerendered and cannot gate anything; there is no service-role key and none may
reach the client bundle; there is no entitlement table, and adding one is new
schema this sprint did not touch.

**Unresolved:** whether Nepal is in scope at all (no provider documents support
for a Nepali payer, and the RBI carve-out could not be read from a
machine-readable source); whether MDR is returned on a refund; chargeback fees
in rupees; Razorpay's contradictory settlement cycle; the GST/OIDAR direction
question, which is for a CA.
